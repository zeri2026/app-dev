# My 3 favorite series is ***Naruto, My Hero Academia, and Black clover***.
###
## 1. Naruto
![Naruto and Kurama](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b81903a2-4710-4ea1-8ca0-a371a07d5c30)
- **Naruto Uzumaki**
    - I love Naruto because he grew up hated and alone, yet he stayed kind and never gave up. He is super loyal to his friends and always keeps his promises, no matter how hard things get. Seeing him understand and save even his worst enemies makes him truly goated.
- **What I see in Naruto**
    - **How loneliness changes people:** What I really love about Naruto is how honestly it explores what happens when someone grows up completely isolated, ignored, or treated like an outcast by the people around them. In real life, people who carry that kind of rejection rarely stay cheerful. They usually become defensive, guarded, or bitter just to protect themselves from getting hurt again, which is why the villains in this show feel like genuinely wounded human beings to me rather than simple cartoon bad guys.
    - **Why letting go is hard:** In my opinion, one of the most realistic parts of the story is how it handles anger and revenge. When someone ruins your life or hurts the people you care about, wanting to get even feels completely justified in the moment, and the series never pretends that moving on is easy. It shows how messy, painful, and uncomfortable it actually is to break a cycle of hatred and choose forgiveness when every part of you wants to fight back.
    - **Having someone in your corner:** To me, the most relatable message in Naruto is that people almost never change their ways just because someone lectures them or tells them they are wrong. Real change usually happens when a stubborn, loyal friend refuses to walk away, listens to your side of the story, and keeps showing up for you even when you are acting at your absolute worst.
###
## 2. My Hero Academia
![Izuku Midoriya](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/480621bc-2a20-4455-acdd-e03d914b8bd6)
- **Izuku Midoriya (Deku)**
    - I like Izuku because he ran in to save someone even before he had any powers, showing he has a true hero's heart. He is smart and trains really hard to control a power that hurts his own body. His willingness to get hurt just to keep others safe makes him easy to cheer for.
- **What I see in My Hero Academia**
    - **Comparing yourself to others:** I relate a lot to how My Hero Academia captures that quiet, everyday frustration of watching naturally gifted people succeed effortlessly while you have to work twice as hard just to stay in the race. It really nails that specific kind of self doubt where you start questioning whether your effort actually matters, or if talent will always beat hard work no matter how much time you put in.
    - **The stress of expectations:** What really stands out to me is how well it portrays the heavy mental pressure of trying to live up to high expectations from parents, teachers, or peers. It shows how exhausting it can be when everyone expects you to have everything figured out, forcing you to hide your stress behind a confident smile because you are terrified of letting down the people who believe in you.
    - **Acting while scared:** I genuinely appreciate how this series redefines what bravery looks like in real life. Instead of making heroism look effortless, it reminds me that you rarely feel fearless before doing something difficult. Most of the time your hands are shaking and you are doubting yourself, and real courage is simply deciding to step up and help anyway despite being terrified.
###
## 3. Black Clover
![Asta](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc097e49-605c-492f-b646-84932a8a8a14)
- **Asta**
    - I respect Asta because he was born with no magic at all in the world where magic is everything, yet he trained his body every day instead of giving up. What makes him so likable is his loud, positive energy even when almost everyone look down on him. Watching him turn insults into strength and lift up his teammates is always awesome.
- **What I see in Black Clover**
    - **Dealing with unfair labels:** One thing I really respect about Black Clover is how directly it reflects the real world frustration of being judged, underestimated, or looked down on simply because of where you come from, how much money you have, or who you know. It captures that feeling of having to work ten times harder just to earn the same basic respect that privileged people get automatically without even trying.
    - **Staying positive when things are tough:** Instead of sitting around complaining about an unfair system or feeling sorry for yourself, I admire how the series shows that choosing to stay upbeat and putting in quiet, consistent effort every single day is actually a very practical survival skill. To me, it proves that keeping a positive attitude is not about ignoring reality. It is about refusing to let other people's low expectations stop you from improving yourself.
    - **Finding people who accept you:** For me, the best part of the whole show is how realistically it shows that trying to prove yourself completely alone will eventually burn you out. Having a group of imperfect, messy friends who understand your struggles, do not judge your flaws, and genuinely celebrate your progress makes it infinitely easier to keep pushing forward when life gets tough.
 
## What All 3 Series Have in Common

| Real-Life Similarity | Naruto | My Hero Academia | Black Clover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dealing With Rejection** | Shows how people mask deep loneliness with loud behavior just to be noticed | Captures the quiet sadness of feeling left out when everyone else moves forward | Reflects the frustration of being looked down on simply because of your background |
| **Comparing Yourself to Talented Peers** | Watching naturally gifted classmates succeed easily while Naruto fails the basic exams | Watching talented peers look effortless while Izuku struggles to control his own body | Watching gifted peers get praised as geniuses while Asta gets looked down on |
| **Turning Pain Into Strength** | Training until exhaustion to prove he is not a failure | Taking notes and pushing past physical pain just to catch up | Not stopping improving the things that he have beside magic |
| **Needing One Person to Believe in You** | Having a teacher finally acknowledge Naruto instead of treating him like an outcast | Having a mentor finally tell Izuku that he actually can achieve his dream | Having a captain pick Asta for a squad when everyone else rejected him |
| **Leading by Example** | Changing stubborn people around him through sheer loyalty and grit | Inspiring people around him to take risks and act bravely during crises | Waking up his lazy, unmotivated teammates so they start taking things seriously |
